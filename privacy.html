Privacy Policy

Split Screen Exercise Timer · Effective September 25, 2026

Support Privacy Policy

The short version

Split Screen Exercise Timer does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data. Everything you create stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

Your workouts (titles, text, and durations) and a few display preferences, such as where you left the split bar and your text size. These are saved only on your device, using Apple's standard on-device storage. They are included in your device backups according to your own iCloud or computer backup settings, which the developer cannot access.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, advertising, or tracking.
  • No data is sent to the developer or any third party. The app does not connect to the internet.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local alerts on your iPhone for the halfway point and the end of a workout. These are created and delivered on your device; no notification data leaves it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete a workout by swiping it in the list, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, for example because a future version adds a feature that sends data off the device, this page will be updated with a new effective date before that version is released. Questions? Open an issue.
